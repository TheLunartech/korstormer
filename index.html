Frequently asked questions

Clear before you commit.

Do I pay directly on the website?

No. Send your request through WhatsApp first. Korstomer confirms availability, requirements and the final amount before sending payment instructions.

How quickly are subscriptions activated?

Activation time depends on the requested service and current availability. You will be given a timeframe before payment.

Do you guarantee advertising results?

No responsible agency can guarantee sales, messages or clicks. We plan, target, monitor and optimize campaigns, while actual performance depends on the market, creative, audience, competition and budget.

Can you create content for my ads?

Yes. Professional photo, video, drone, editing, voice-over and jingle production are available as separate quoted services.

Where is Korstomer located?

Santa Maria, Accra, Ghana. Most enquiries and onboarding can also be handled remotely through WhatsApp and email.
